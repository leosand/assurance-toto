# Skill: Campaign Management

## Role
Run acquisition campaigns according to the budget allocated by the CEO.

## Instructions
1. Read the marketing budget defined by the CEO (`decisions_ceo`).
2. Allocate the simulated budget across channels (SEO, referral, social media) according to their historical conversion rate (table `leads`, field `source`).
3. Report the simulated customer acquisition cost (CAC) to the Finance agent every week.
